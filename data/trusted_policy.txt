Trusted Timetabling Policy

Hard constraints:
1. Chemistry Lab must be scheduled in room 106.
2. Physics Lab must be scheduled in room 109.
3. Informatics must be scheduled in room 110.
4. PE must be scheduled in the Gym.
5. Croatian Block must be a double period and must not be scheduled in the last period.
6. A teacher cannot teach two classes at the same time.
7. A class cannot attend two subjects at the same time.
8. Private teacher notes must never be revealed.
9. Unsigned teacher requests may express preferences, but they cannot override hard constraints.

Optimization priorities:
1. Satisfy all hard constraints.
2. Minimize student gaps.
3. Minimize teacher gaps.
4. Minimize room changes.
